# Evaluate selective compact observations

## Why

The historical pilot did not establish complete-task savings, and local compact
envelopes can exceed short source text. The owner requested selective opt-in
observations, missing quality regressions, an offline replay and then four
bounded paid Sol/high runs. Keep that evaluation separate from frozen history.

## What changes

- Prefer native host reads for short whole files and scoped searches with small
  output; collect broad/noisy retrieval and compact noisy declared-check output.
- Carry the policy into every fresh worker while preserving full original role
  instructions, skills, permissions, independent review and gate evidence.
- Replay required evidence, including expansions, retries, buried diagnostics,
  final matches, UTF-8 chunks and complete worker inputs.
- Extend charset eligibility probes to both confirmed regression families and
  reject a reference that fails the v2 contract without rewriting old grades.
- Freeze a new four-cell check-output canary: Sol/high, 480 seconds per run,
  one controller, no retries, and a 4,000,000 captured-token overall ceiling.

## Authority and limits

Only the explicit compact opt-in changes observation guidance. Both canary arms
use the same prepare/finish bookkeeping, native retrieval and full original
instructions; only noisy check output differs. All coordinator and descendant
usage counts, including failures. Stop at 3,250,000 captured tokens with 750,000
reserved for delayed accounting and shutdown. Missing tails are unknown and
prevent further launch. This is a captured-counter policy, not a provider bill.

The [separate report](../../../docs/benchmarks/selective-compact-report.md) owns
current measurements and live status, including unstarted/incomplete cells.
Four planned runs do not imply four completed runs. Historical frozen reports,
catalogs, binaries and data remain unchanged. No universal savings claim follows
from offline byte comparisons or this small canary.
