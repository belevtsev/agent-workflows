## Observation policy

Keep the existing `--compact` switch. Prefer native host reads for short whole
files and scoped native search when output is small. Use typed context commands
for broad/noisy retrieval and independent batches. Compact noisy declared checks
and expand retained logs until the evidence needed for a decision is present.
No mandatory routing of every read/command and no component flag is introduced.

The collector caches local rendered text, independently of a provider's prompt
cache. Every needed expansion, fallback and retry contributes to transport.
Original full role instructions and selected skill bodies stay uncompressed.
Private artifacts grant no tools, review approval, check pass or completed work.

## Evaluation boundaries

Replay the final needed search match, long UTF-8 evidence, a diagnostic outside
the preview, native short reads and complete worker inputs. Report increases as
well as decreases. Byte equivalence does not establish model-quality equivalence.
The v2 charset contract includes extended-parameter overrides and legacy
unquoted stray quotes, preserving literal ordinary charset policy. A rejected
reference stays rejected for v2, and historical grades are not edited.

The separate `selective-canary-v1` profile has two tasks, one repetition, two
arms, one controller, `gpt-6.1-sol`/`high` and 480 seconds per run. Both arms use
native retrieval and identical managed bookkeeping. Only noisy declared-check
output differs, with retained-log expansion allowed in the compact arm.
Short Go check output remains ordinary in both arms; the noisy JavaScript suite
uses the assigned ordinary or compact check output.

The overall captured ceiling is 4,000,000 tokens: stop at 3,250,000 and reserve
750,000 for delayed counters/shutdown, also requiring reserve before launching.
Count unique cumulative input plus output across every coordinator/descendant;
cached input and reasoning remain subsets. Missing counters, incomplete results
or reconciliation disagreements stop further launches. Retain failed identities
and never retry. Report unstarted and incomplete cells explicitly. Discrete
counter polling leaves in-flight tails unknown and is not a hard billing cap.
