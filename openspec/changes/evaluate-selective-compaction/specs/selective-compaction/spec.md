# Selective compact evaluation

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Selective opt-in observations

Explicit compact mode SHALL prefer native short whole-file reads and scoped
native searches with small output, use collection for broad/noisy retrieval and
bounded output for noisy declared checks, and permit retained-evidence expansion.
Plain task-start SHALL remain unchanged. Every fresh worker SHALL retain full
original instructions, skills, permissions and independent review requirements.

Source: owner-authorized item 4 and proposal.md.

#### Scenario: Plain task start

- **WHEN** the caller omits compact opt-in on either supported host
- **THEN** the original handoff prompt remains unchanged.

#### Scenario: Fresh selective worker

- **WHEN** an opted-in coordinator prepares a fresh worker
- **THEN** its original instructions and selective observation policy remain present without new role authority.

### Requirement: Complete offline evidence replay

Offline replay SHALL compare the same required facts and charge all requests,
responses, needed expansions, rejected requests, retries, dispatch envelopes,
complete saved briefs and original selected-skill reads. It SHALL distinguish
transport bytes and local cache reuse from provider tokens and prompt caching.

Source: owner-authorized item 6 and proposal.md.

#### Scenario: Evidence outside a preview

- **WHEN** a required match, UTF-8 fact or check diagnostic is outside the initial page
- **THEN** replay recovers it and charges every expansion or retry needed for equivalence.

### Requirement: Charset v2 eligibility

The separate v2 contract SHALL cover extended-parameter override and legacy
unquoted stray-quote regressions alongside the original task. Missing executed
evidence or a failure in either family SHALL prevent v2 eligibility. Historical
frozen grades and reports SHALL remain unchanged.

Source: owner-authorized item 5 and proposal.md.

#### Scenario: Incomplete or failing v2 evidence

- **WHEN** a reference fails a v2 behavior or execution evidence is missing
- **THEN** it cannot serve as v2 ground truth and the separate result preserves the failure.

### Requirement: Bounded four-cell canary

The new canary SHALL freeze four planned Sol/high runs across two paired tasks,
480 seconds each, with one controller and no retry. Both arms SHALL retain
identical managed bookkeeping, native retrieval and full original instructions;
only noisy declared-check output SHALL differ. It SHALL preserve the historical
study profile and reject incompatible canary settings.

Source: owner's four-run authorization and proposal.md.

#### Scenario: Canary profile validation

- **WHEN** a manifest selects the separate canary profile
- **THEN** the harness enforces its bounded settings without weakening the historical study validation.

### Requirement: Cumulative captured accounting

The canary SHALL count unique coordinator and descendant cumulative input plus
output across all planned cells against 4,000,000 captured tokens, stopping at
3,250,000 with a 750,000 reserve. Missing counters, incomplete tails or accounting
disagreement SHALL prevent further launch. Failed, unstarted and incomplete
cells SHALL remain visible; unknown usage SHALL NOT become zero or a savings claim.

Source: owner's overall captured-token ceiling and proposal.md.

#### Scenario: Captured budget stop

- **WHEN** cumulative usage reaches the stop threshold or accounting becomes incomplete
- **THEN** the controller stops its local descendant processes and blocks further launches.

#### Scenario: Incomplete planned cells

- **WHEN** the run budget or interruption prevents a planned cell from completing
- **THEN** reporting preserves its incomplete or unstarted status and any captured lower bound without retry.
