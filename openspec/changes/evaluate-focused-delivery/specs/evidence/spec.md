# Revised delivery evidence

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Frozen approved comparison

Preparation SHALL freeze the revised source/binary, original behavior, roles,
guides, oracle and order. Execution SHALL remain within the explicitly approved
pair/budget and preserve original quality and Git requirements. Historical
assets MUST remain unchanged. Ordinary tests MUST invoke no model.

Source: owner request and approved comparison plan.

#### Scenario: Offline preparation and eligibility

- **WHEN** the revised comparison is prepared
- **THEN** a new private root and modified binary identity are retained, the baseline fails the frozen behavior oracle and the reference passes, without a paid call.

### Requirement: Actual complete solver usage

Every unique coordinator and descendant SHALL be counted once using actual
cumulative counters, with cached input and reasoning treated as subsets.
Completed execution SHALL require final native session records and correct
models. Incomplete evidence MUST remain unknown and prevent another launch.

Source: owner-approved all-worker accounting and reserve policy.

#### Scenario: Final tail and missing worker

- **WHEN** a native session is unfinished or a required worker is missing
- **THEN** completed accounting is refused and later model launches remain blocked.

### Requirement: Separate quality and honest interpretation

The report SHALL separate frozen acceptance, original regressions, integrity,
workflow evidence, protocol certainty and accounting completeness. It SHALL
include uncached/cached categories, worker attribution and limitations. Neither
byte reductions nor a single passing pair MAY become proof of general quality
equivalence, a lower bill or exact encrypted dispatch equality.

Source: approved plan and verification-article assessment.

#### Scenario: Sanitized paired evidence

- **WHEN** posthoc results are exported
- **THEN** identities, every actual worker counter and the shared total reconcile, while unsupported quality/protocol/cost claims remain unknown.
